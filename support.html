常見問題

怎麼邀請家人朋友？
打開群組設定，把 6 位數邀請碼傳給對方。對方在 App 裡選「用邀請碼加入」並輸入這 6 位數。

「接任務」是什麼？
給「任何人」的單次待辦，要先按「接任務」才能做，避免兩個人做同一件事。接了不想做可以按「放棄」。

多人合作怎麼用？
發布時打開「多人合作」並設定需要幾個人（2～6 人）。大家按「加入」，人到齊後任何一位送出，發布者確認後每個人都拿到點數。

沒有網路可以用嗎？
可以看和操作，連上網路後會自動同步。接任務、加入多人待辦需要連網，才不會和別人搶到同一個。

怎麼刪除帳號？
在 App 的「設定」裡選「刪除帳號」。詳見隱私權政策。

買了進階版，換手機怎麼辦？
在新手機用同一個 Apple 帳號登入 App Store，進入購買頁按「恢復購買」。

FAQ

How do I invite people?
Open group settings and send the 6-digit invite code. They choose “Join with Code” in the app and enter it.

What does “Take On” mean?
One-time to-dos for anyone must be taken on before they can be done, so two people don’t do the same job. Tap “Give Up” if you change your mind.

How do team to-dos work?
Turn on “Team To-Do” when posting and choose 2–6 people. Others tap “Join”; once the team is full anyone can send it in, and when the poster confirms, everyone earns the points.

Does it work offline?
Yes, changes sync when you are back online. Taking a to-do and joining a team need a connection so two people can’t grab the same spot.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy.

I bought Premium — what about a new phone?
Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, open the purchase page and tap “Restore Purchases”.
