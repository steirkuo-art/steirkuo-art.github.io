隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

DuoQuest（以下稱「本 App」）由個人開發者提供。我們只收集讓本 App 運作所需的資料，不放廣告、不追蹤你、不出售或分享你的資料給廣告商或資料仲介。

我們收集的資料

  • 帳號資料：登入用的 Email；使用 Google 登入時，Google 帳號提供的顯示名稱。
  • 暱稱與帳號編號：你在 App 裡設定的暱稱，以及系統給每個帳號的編號。
  • 你建立的內容：群組名稱、待辦、備註、留言、獎勵卡、點數與兌換紀錄。
  • 照片：大頭照、發布待辦時附的照片、完成待辦時拍的照片。

我們不收集位置、通訊錄、健康資料、廣告識別碼，也沒有使用分析或當機回報工具。

資料怎麼使用

只用來提供本 App 的功能：登入、讓同一群組的成員看到彼此的待辦和點數、同步多支手機、防止重複領點數。

誰看得到

你的暱稱、大頭照和你在群組裡建立的內容，只有同一個群組的成員看得到。加入群組需要 6 位數邀請碼。本 App 沒有公開頁面，也沒有和陌生人聊天的功能。

資料存放在哪裡

資料存放在 Google 提供的 Firebase 服務（Firebase Authentication 與 Cloud Firestore，伺服器位於台灣）。Google 依照其服務條款代為處理資料，見 Firebase 隱私權與安全性說明。

保存多久

  • 完成待辦時拍的照片：發布者確認或退回後，會從雲端刪除。
  • 其他資料：保存到你退出群組、群組被刪除，或你刪除帳號為止。
  • 退出群組或刪除帳號後，其他成員那邊已完成的紀錄（例如誰完成了什麼待辦）可能會保留，作為他們的點數紀錄。

刪除帳號

你可以隨時在 App 的「設定」裡刪除帳號。刪除後，你的帳號、暱稱、大頭照和你建立的內容會從雲端移除（上一點提到的紀錄除外）。

兒童

本 App 可以讓家人一起使用，包括孩子。我們不會向孩子收集超過上面列出的資料。建議 13 歲以下的孩子由家長協助建立帳號並一起使用。家長如需刪除孩子的資料，可以在 App 裡刪除帳號，或寫信給我們。

App 內購買

進階版透過 Apple 的 App 內購買付款。付款資料由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的信用卡或付款資訊。

政策變更

如果政策有變更，我們會更新這個頁面和上方的生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

DuoQuest (“the app”) is made by an independent developer. We collect only what the app needs to work. There are no ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

What we collect

  • Account data: the email you sign in with; if you sign in with Google, the display name from your Google account.
  • Nickname and account ID: the nickname you choose and the ID assigned to your account.
  • Content you create: group names, to-dos, notes, messages, reward cards, points and redemption history.
  • Photos: your profile photo, photos attached to posted to-dos, and photos taken when completing a to-do.

We do not collect location, contacts, health data or advertising identifiers, and we use no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

How we use it

Only to run the app: signing in, showing group members each other’s to-dos and points, syncing across phones, and preventing points from being counted twice.

Who can see it

Your nickname, profile photo and the content you create in a group are visible only to members of that group, who join with a 6-digit invite code. The app has no public pages and no chat with strangers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, servers in Taiwan), which processes it on our behalf under its terms. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

How long we keep it

  • Photos taken when completing a to-do are deleted from the cloud once the poster approves or sends it back.
  • Everything else is kept until you leave the group, the group is deleted, or you delete your account.
  • After you leave or delete your account, other members may keep records of completed to-dos as part of their points history.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app’s Settings. Your account, nickname, profile photo and the content you created are then removed from the cloud, except for the records noted above.

Children

Families, including children, can use the app together. We collect nothing from children beyond what is listed above. We recommend that a parent set up and use the app with children under 13. Parents can delete a child’s data by deleting the account in the app or by emailing us.

In-app purchases

Premium is paid through Apple’s in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
